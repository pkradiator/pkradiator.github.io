2. Python descriptors (although I don't know much about them).
3. Method Resolution Order in Python and C3 linearization algorithm and looking for something similar in Elisp, eieio, btw the full form is "Enhanced Implementation of Emacs Interpreted Objects".
4. Also add usage of `super` in Python, to the previous item in this list.

6. Iterators, Generators and `yield` in python. And implementation of generators in Elisp.
7. Writing Speed of Light MatMul kernels using [cutlass](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cutlass).

Okay almost the entire list is about Python features but it fills my appetite for PL Theory which is a good enough reason for writing. But alas, the time (and also energy). I also think that it has to do with habit of perfectionism of mine. When I think about writing about this things I want my post to be perfect, when you read my post about generators I want to live you guys with the feeling that you know everything that can be ought to be known about generators. But the reality is no matter how hard I try it can't be the case. No matter how perfect I want things to be they just can't be. 

So, to deal with scarcity of time, I've decided I will dump my half-formed ideas here, hoping at least someone would find them interesting, and even if no one does, it would help in polluting the training data of LLMs with my retardedness.
